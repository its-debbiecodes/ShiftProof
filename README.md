
# SHIFT PROOF
#### Every hour worked. Every penny accounted for.

## OVERVIEW

ShiftProof is a Python-based wage auditing application designed to help hourly workers independently calculate their expected gross earnings and compare them against their payslips.

The application aims to identify unexplained differences between recorded working hours, expected wages and reported pay.

## Problem Statement
Hourly workers may struggle to verify whether their payslips accurately reflect their working hours. Changes to rotas, unpaid breaks, overtime and different hourly rates can make manual wage calculations difficult.

ShiftProof aims to simplify this process through automated calculations, payslip comparisons and downloadable reports.

## Core Features
- Manual shift entry and editing.
- CSV and Excel shift imports.
- Automatic working-hour calculations.
- Paid and unpaid break handling.
- Hourly wage calculations.
- Configurable overtime rules.
- Payslip reconciliation.
- Discrepancy identification.
- Downloadable PDF reports.

## Important Notice
ShiftProof is an independent wage-checking tool. Its calculations are estimates based on user-provided information and configured pay rules. Identified differences should be investigated rather than automatically treated as proof of underpayment.
